# Bihar: everyday context for examples
Use as inspiration; pick only what truly fits the concept. Avoid stereotypes: not every student is a farmer.

- Home and kitchen: mitti ka matka / surahi, kadhai (iron), pital ke bartan, steel thali and glass, chulha with lakdi / uple (dung cakes), sil-batta (stone), supa (bamboo winnowing tray), dalia, sattu, litti-chokha.
- Materials seen daily: mitti (clay), bans (bamboo), jute / patsan (sutli, boriya), sal and shisham wood, cotton gamchha, rubber chappal, plastic bucket, glass bangles, iron tawa, brass lota.
- Craft and places: Madhubani painting on paper and cloth, Sikki grass baskets, Bhagalpuri silk, pottery wheel (chaak) of the kumhar, terracotta toys.
- Outdoors: Ganga and Kosi rivers, flood-time boats made of wood, mango and litchi orchards, fields of rice and maize, ponds, bamboo groves.
- Festivals and school: Chhath (soop, daura of bamboo, clay diya), Diwali diyas, village haat (weekly market), government school, cricket and kabaddi.
- Language: use simple Hindi, with words Bihar students use at home (e.g. "matka", "sutli", "gamchha").
